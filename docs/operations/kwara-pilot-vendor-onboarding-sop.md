# PropVendor Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
## Kwara State Pilot: Vendor Onboarding & Asset Verification

**Document Reference:** `PV-SOP-OPS-19-3-01`  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** October 29, 2026  
**Target Pilot Region:** Kwara State (Primary Focus: Ilorin Metropolitan Area — GRA, Fate, Tanke, University Road, Ganmo, Eiyenkorin)  
**Classification:** Operational / Confidential  
**Milestone:** Phase 19.3 — Initial Real Inventory Supply  

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) governs the identification, vetting, onboarding, and listing intake of real estate brokers, private property developers, licensed surveyors, and automotive dealerships in Kwara State during the PropVendor Phase 19.3 pilot.

The objective is to seed the PropVendor marketplace exclusively with **100% genuine, permission-based, and physically inspected inventory**, while enforcing consumer safety, anti-advance-fee mandates, and Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) privacy standards.

---

### 2.2 Automotive Dealership Onboarding Protocol
Automotive dealers marketing vehicles on PropVendor must operate a physical dealership or commercial lot:

1. **Physical Dealership Verification:** Field verification of physical lot/showroom in Ilorin with signage and permanent business presence.
2. **Business Registration:** CAC Certificate of Registration indicating automotive sales/trading.
3. **Authorized Representative Identification:** Valid National Identity Number (NIN) slip or FRSC Driver's License of the dealer principal or sales manager.
4. **Customs & Documentation Commitment:** Written undertaking that every imported vehicle (Tokunbo) possesses genuine Nigerian Customs Service Single Goods Declaration (SGD) clearance, and every Nigerian-used vehicle has verified FRSC/VIO registration records.

## 4. Photographic Evidence & Geotagging Standards

Every listing must be accompanied by original, high-resolution photographs meeting the following specifications:
1. **Camera Settings:** Minimum resolution of `1024 x 768` pixels. Geolocation/GPS metadata enabled where feasible.
2. **Daylight Requirement:** All photos must be captured between 08:00 and 17:30 WAT under natural illumination. Zero night-time or flash-only photography.
3. **Required Angles:**
   * **Properties:** Exterior facade with street view, living room, master bedroom, kitchen, bathrooms, and compound/parking area.
   * **Land Parcels:** Wide panorama from boundary front, photos of all boundary beacons, access road approaching plot, and surrounding landmark structures.
   * **Vehicles:** Front 45-degree angle, rear 45-degree angle, interior dashboard with odometer reading, engine bay, trunk/cargo area, and close-up of VIN dash plate.
4. **Watermark Prohibition:** No watermarks from third-party platforms (Jiji, PropertyPro, NigeriaPropertyCentre). Zero superimposed contact telephone numbers or WhatsApp logos.

## 5. KW-GIS Cross-Referencing Operational Guidelines

When reviewing land parcels or newly developed estates in Ilorin, the operations team must adhere to the following KW-GIS search guidelines:

1. **Cadastral Identification:** Request the coordinate data (UTM Minna Datum, Zone 31N or 32N) from the vendor's registered survey plan.
2. **Government Reservation Check:** Determine if the parcel falls within Kwara State Government designated reserves (e.g., Ilorin Master Plan greenbelts, Airport expansion safety corridors, or University of Ilorin buffer zones).
3. **Excision Status:** In communal land transactions (e.g., Eiyenkorin, Ganmo, Oke-Oyi), confirm whether the community has obtained a formal State Government Excision Gazette.
4. **Consumer Protection Disclosure:** Every land listing approved on PropVendor must display the standard notice informing buyers that title must be independently verified at the Kwara State Geographic Information Service before consideration funds are released.
